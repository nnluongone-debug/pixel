## 🇺🇸 English (en-US)

### App name (21/30)
Habit Tracker - Pixel

### Short description (≤80)
Build better habits one pixel brick at a time. Keep streaks, level up, unlock!

### Full description
🧱 EVERY HABIT YOU COMPLETE DROPS A PIXEL BRICK

Habit Tracker - Pixel turns building habits into a retro arcade game. Each time you finish a habit, a glowing neon brick drops into your pixel matrix. Day by day, the bricks stack into a picture of your own progress — and you'll want to keep it growing.

✨ HIGHLIGHTS

🟩 A living pixel matrix
• Each column is a day, each brick a completed habit
• Satisfying brick-drop animation and arcade sound effects
• See at a glance how strong your week really was

⚡ Create habits in seconds
• Start typing and get ready-made templates — Drink water, Read, Meditate, Run, Sleep early… one tap fills in the icon, color, schedule and goal
• Track it your way: Pages, Minutes, Times, Glasses or a simple Check
• Flexible schedules: daily, weekdays, or pick your own days
• Built-in focus timer for minute-based habits

⏰ Smart reminders
• Up to 5 reminder times a day
• Or repeat every 15 min – 3 h within a time window (e.g. drink water every hour, 8 AM–10 PM)

🏆 Level up & earn badges
• Earn XP for every check-in, with a bonus for keeping your streak
• Level up and unlock tiered badges
• Weekly/monthly stats, best streak and completion rate

🎨 Brick theme vault
• 6 themes: Game Boy, Neon Glow, Cyberpunk, Gemstone, Pure Gold, Nebula
• Each comes with its own effects and sound pack
• Unlock them with levels and streaks — the more consistent you are, the cooler it looks

📱 Home screen widget
• See your matrix and check off habits right from your home screen (iOS)

☁️ Safe sync
• Works instantly, no account needed
• Sign in with email + 6-digit code (no password) to back up and sync across devices
• Works offline, syncs when you're back online
• Delete your account and all data anytime, right in the app

🌐 Available in English and Vietnamese

No ads. No spam. Just you and your daily pixel bricks.
